# 🚀 ServiceSphere

**ServiceSphere** is a versatile and user-friendly platform designed to provide quick access to four core services — **Mechanic Assistance**, **House Rentals**, **Car Rentals**, and **Emergency Medical Assistance**. In addition to its service offerings, ServiceSphere empowers individuals to monetize their **skills and talents** by offering them as services on the platform.

## 🌐 Project Overview

ServiceSphere is dedicated to enhancing the digital service experience by:
- Ensuring **effortless navigation** across all features.
- Providing **quick access to vital services** for users in urgent or everyday scenarios.
- Creating **opportunities for skilled individuals** to reach a broader audience and earn through their service offerings.

## 🛠️ Features

- 🔧 **Mechanic Assistance**: Connects users with nearby professional mechanics in case of vehicle breakdowns.
- 🏠 **House Rentals**: Enables users to find and list rental properties with ease.
- 🚗 **Car Rentals**: Lets users rent cars for short or long durations at affordable rates.
- 🏥 **Emergency Medical Assistance**: Offers immediate support and resources during medical emergencies.
- 💼 **Service Provider Module**: Individuals can register, list their skills (plumbing, electrician work, personal tutoring, etc.), and get hired directly through the platform.

## ☁️ Cloud Infrastructure

- 💻 **EC2 (Elastic Compute Cloud)**: Used to host and deploy the platform, ensuring high availability and scalability for seamless performance.
- 🧩 AWS integration ensures robust backend performance and optimized content delivery.

## 📱 Tech Stack (Suggestive)
- **Frontend**: HTML,CSS,Js
- **Database**: Local Storage
- **Cloud**: AWS (EC2, S3, etc.)
